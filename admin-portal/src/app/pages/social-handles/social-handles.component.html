📸 How to Post to Instagram

  1. Export PNG — Click "⬇️ Export PNG" to download the poster image.
  2. Copy caption — Click "📋 Copy" to copy the formatted caption.
  3. Open Instagram — Tap +, select your image, paste the caption.
  4. Add hashtags & location for better reach.
  5. Post! — Use 9:16 for Stories/Reels; 1:1 for Feed posts.
💡 Tip: Post at 7–9 PM IST on weekdays for best engagement.
